## What Saydle does

Saydle shows you a short daily affirmation, lets you practise reading affirmations aloud, and, on a paid plan, writes affirmations from the answers you give about yourself. To do that we collect and process the information below.

## Information you give us

- **Account details:** your email address, a password (stored only as a one-way hash), and the name you want to be called.
- **Personalisation answers** from onboarding and the Profile screen. Some of these are sensitive: how you have been feeling, what supports your wellbeing (including whether you attend therapy), your relationship situation, your faith or belief tradition, your age range, and free-text answers about what you are working on or what is weighing on you. Every sensitive question can be skipped, and you can change or clear answers at any time in Profile.
- **Your own affirmations** ("My words") that you write in the app.
- **Preferences:** tone, categories, theme, reminder times, chosen voice, language and time zone.

## Information collected automatically

- **Product activity:** which affirmations you have seen or favourited, your streak, and your position in the library. This is what makes the app work across devices.
- **Subscription status** from Apple or Google via RevenueCat: whether you are subscribed, which plan, and when it renews or ended. We never see your card or Apple ID payment details.
- **Diagnostic logs** on our servers containing request paths, timestamps and status codes. We do not keep IP addresses or browser identifiers in these logs.

## What stays on your device

Recordings you make of yourself reading an affirmation are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

## How we use your information

- To run your account and show you your affirmations, favourites and streak.
- To generate affirmations for you. On the paid plan, your tone, categories, focus, values, faith or belief tradition, age range, and free-text answers are sent to Google Cloud's Vertex AI to write them. Your name and email are not sent. Answers about mood, therapy, relationships and anything that reads as a crisis are never sent; they only soften the tone of what is written.
- To read affirmations aloud. On the paid plan, the text of an affirmation (including your own words) is sent to ElevenLabs to produce audio. Your name is removed first.
- To send you emails you asked for: verification codes, password reset codes, and notices about account deletion. These are sent through Resend.
- To honour your subscription, using the events Apple or Google send us through RevenueCat.
- To keep the service secure and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

## Legal basis

Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 or the GDPR applies: running your account is performance of a contract; sending the answers you chose to give to our AI providers is based on your consent, which you give by answering and can withdraw by clearing the answer; security and abuse prevention are our legitimate interests. Faith, health and similar answers are processed only with your explicit consent, which is why every one of those questions can be skipped.

## How long we keep it

- Your account data is kept while your account exists.
- When you delete your account there is a **30-day grace period** during which you can keep the account by signing in and tapping "Keep my account". After that everything above is permanently removed.
- A minimal deletion record is kept for **[six] years**: a hashed form of your email, the account's creation and deletion dates, and your subscription history (plan, dates, store). It contains no answers, affirmations or name. We keep it to handle refund and billing disputes and to meet record-keeping obligations. [Confirm the period with counsel.]
- Verification and reset codes expire within 24 hours.

## Your rights

You can see and change your answers and preferences in the app. You can delete your account from Profile → Delete account. You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, object to processing, or complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or your local supervisory authority. Write to [support@saydle.app] and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

Saydle is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app.
